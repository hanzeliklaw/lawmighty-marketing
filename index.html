Get paid · built on LawPay

From time entry to paid invoice, without leaving LawMighty.

Generate the invoice, send it, and accept payment — card or bank (eCheck) — through LawPay. Your clients open their portal, see exactly what was billed, and pay it there. Invoice payments go to operating, retainers go to trust, and every payment records itself.

In the client portal

Pay now, on the invoice

Clients open an invoice in their portal, see exactly what was billed, and pay it by card or bank (eCheck). Before a bank payment, LawPay shows its standard authorization. If the firm hasn't set up eCheck, the client sees a clear notice. The payment lands on that invoice the moment it's accepted.

From an email

Send a LawPay link

One click on any invoice emails the client a LawPay link with the amount locked. Card or bank (eCheck). When they pay, LawMighty matches it and marks the invoice.

Retainers

Straight to trust

Request a retainer by card or bank (eCheck) and it goes to your trust account on LawPay's side — and onto the client's IOLTA ledger here only once it settles. It cannot be routed anywhere else.

Built the way a lawyer would insist on

  • LawMighty never touches the money. Funds move between your client and your own LawPay accounts under your agreement with LawPay.
  • Card and bank details never reach us. They're handled by LawPay's secure fields; we record the outcome.
  • LawPay's authorization, before a bank payment. Paying by bank (eCheck) shows the standard LawPay authorization first.
  • Save a bank account and turn on autopay. Clients can keep a bank account on file and turn autopay on.
  • A clear notice if eCheck isn't set up. If the firm hasn't set up eCheck, the client sees a clear notice.
  • Separate trust and operating accounts. Invoice payments go to operating and retainers go to trust. You map each account once; the two can't be crossed.
  • Refunds and voids show as refunded. A refund or void done in LawPay shows as refunded, and appears as its own line in firm records and the client portal.
  • Settled means settled. Every status you see comes from LawPay's own record, checked every hour.

Already on LawPay? Connect with LawPay from Firm Settings in about a minute. Not yet? We'll point you to the sign-up.

The part no incumbent ships

Discovery is where small firms bleed hours. We built two tools for it.

Both came out of a working family-law caseload, not a product roadmap. Both are included with every firm account. Neither sends anything out the door without an attorney reviewing it first.

Outgoing discovery

MightyDiscovery™

Upload the complaint. Get a first draft of interrogatories and requests for production, each tied to something actually alleged in the pleadings — ready for your edits, not a generic template.

  • Drafted from your pleadings and any supporting documents you add
  • Optionally matched to the style of a set you've served before
  • Edit, exclude, or reorder every question before anything is generated
  • Caption, signature block, certificate of service and notary jurat filled from your firm settings
  • Exports as a court-ready Word document — no AI branding on anything you file
Incoming production

MightyAnalyzer™

Upload what came back — the other side's responses in a civil case, or the State's discovery in a criminal one. Clean PDFs, scanned PDFs, photos, legacy Word files, large scanned productions included. Get back what's missing, what's evasive, and what deserves a follow-up.

  • Reads scanned and photographed documents, not just text PDFs
  • Built for civil discovery responses and criminal-defense discovery alike
  • Flags deficient, evasive and non-responsive answers request by request
  • Lines up what was asked against what was actually produced
  • Ask it your own questions about a production in plain English
  • Output is a working draft for the attorney — you decide what becomes a deficiency letter or a motion
Responses, tooSend incoming requests to your client through the portal. They answer online; you review and generate the response document.
